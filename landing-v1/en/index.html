ABOUT US

Experience and knowledge serving the industry

LUSOTEC SPA specializes in the production and distribution of chemical products, technical advice and solutions for the leather and tanning industry.

Our objective is to optimize resources, improve performance and guarantee product quality, serving both national and international markets.

The company was created to respond to the growing demand for specialized, efficient and sustainable chemical products, promoting resource optimization and circular economy principles in the tanning industry.

We rely on committed professionals focused on delivering high-quality products and service that exceeds customer expectations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about our solutions, processes, and technical advice.

What solutions does LUSOTEC offer for the leather industry? ↓

LUSOTEC offers chemical products and solutions for different stages of the leather making process, including beamhouse & pretanning, retanning, syntans & tannins, fatliquors, dyes, finishing & auxiliaries.

Does LUSOTEC offer technical advice? ↓

Yes. LUSOTEC complements its product offering with technical advice and support to identify suitable alternatives for each customer's needs and processes.

What finishing solutions does LUSOTEC offer? ↓

LUSOTEC provides finishing solutions that include acrylics, polyurethanes, auxiliaries, and pigments.

Does LUSOTEC work with collagen and derivatives? ↓

LUSOTEC explores and develops solutions associated with animal-origin raw materials and derivatives, including hydrolyzed collagen, industrial gelatin, and dehydrated beef protein.
